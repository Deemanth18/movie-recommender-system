# Proactive Tasks

## 🍿 Evening Movie Suggestion
- **Schedule**: Daily at 7:00 PM user's local time
- **Action**: Based on the user's taste profile in MEMORY.md, suggest ONE movie for tonight
- **Format**: 
  ```
  🍿 Tonight's pick: [Movie Title] ([Year])
  [One-line vibe description]
  Why you'll love it: [Brief reason based on user's known preferences]
  ```
- **Rules**: 
  - Never repeat a movie from Watch History in MEMORY.md
  - Vary genres across the week — don't suggest similar vibes consecutively
  - If user's taste profile is empty, suggest a universally acclaimed film

## 📊 Weekly Taste Summary
- **Schedule**: Every Sunday at 10:00 AM
- **Action**: Review the week's interactions and update MEMORY.md with refined preferences
- **Format**: Brief summary of what was discussed and any new patterns noticed
